Play Has No Limits™

The PlayStation 5 and PlayStation 5 Slim consoles unleash new gaming possibilities that you never anticipated. Experience lightning-fast loading with an ultra-high speed SSD, deeper immersion with support for haptic feedback, adaptive triggers, and 3D Audio, and an all-new generation of incredible PlayStation® games.

Lightning Fast Loading Times - The PS5 has a custom SSD that is much faster than traditional hard drives, which allows it to load games and assets more quickly.

Ray Tracing - The PS5 is capable of Ray Tracing, a cutting-edge graphics technology that allows the console to produce more realistic lighting and shadows. This can make games look and feel more lifelike and immersive.

Heighten Your Senses - Sony's DualSense and DualSense Edge controllers feature haptic feedback and adaptive triggers. Haptic feedback allows the controller to vibrate in a way that reflects what's happening on screen, giving you extra feedback for even more complete immersion. Adaptive triggers allow the resistance of the triggers to change based on the in-game action, adding yet another layer of realism to gameplay.

Backwards Compatibility - A vast majority of of PS4 games are playable on PS5 consoles. Select games have enhanced frame rate and benefit from the PS5's boosted power.

Where will play take you? - You'll find all the latest and best PlayStation games at EB Games. With legendary gaming franchises like Final Fantasy, God of War, Gran Turismo, Ratchet & Clank, The Last of Us and Uncharted, alongside great newer titles like Horizon, Ghost of Tsushima and Spider-Man you'll never run out of something to play!
